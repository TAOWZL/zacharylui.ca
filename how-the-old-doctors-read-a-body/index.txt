53:T462,Why does that matter clinically, and not just as a nice image? Because if the channel is a river, everything you know about rivers applies. A blockage downstream backs water up for miles. Chapter 8 of the Su Wen, the Ling Lan Mi Dian Lun, usually translated as the Secret Treatise of the Numinous Orchid, describes the organs as offices of a royal court. The heart is the sovereign, and clarity of spirit issues from it. The lung is the chancellor, keeping order and rhythm. The liver is the general, and planning comes out of it. Getting from there to a birth chart in a treatment room is a different matter, and I want to be honest about the join. I can date the pieces. What I can’t hand you is a paper trail showing medical lineages carrying the birth chart into clinical practice across those centuries. What I have is how my teachers worked and how I was taught to work. That’s a practice inheritance, not a documented one, and I’d rather say so than dress it up. Youngest map of the three, actually, and still the one the modern curriculum is most embarrassed by."}]
