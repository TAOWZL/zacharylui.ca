Script

If you run a business and your money will not move, no matter how hard you push, this video is for you. I am going to tell you why, and it is probably not what your business coach told you. I have spent 18 years reading qi, the energy that moves through a body, and here is what I keep finding. When somebody's money will not move, it is almost never a strategy problem. It is a flow problem. By the end of this video you will know which of the three blocks is sitting on your money, and what to actually do about it. If you appreciate this kind of content, gently tap that like button. Let's get into it.

So let me start with the thing that sounds strange and then makes complete sense. In the tradition I come from, everything is qi. Your body is qi. Your energy is qi. And money, money is just qi at a bigger scale. It moves the same way water moves, the same way breath moves. It wants to flow. And there is one rule that runs all of it. Qi that stops moving, stagnates. In the body, stuck qi becomes tension, pain, a knot you can feel. In a business, stuck qi becomes money that will not come, deals that stall, a bank balance you do not want to look at. Same thing, bigger channel. So when your money is stuck, you do not have a marketing problem. You have a stagnation problem. And you cannot out-hustle a blocked channel. You have probably already tried. So let me show you the three ways the channel gets blocked, because once you can see yours, you can finally do something about it.

All right, first one. I call this one the Grip. This is the person whose money will not move because they are holding on too tight. The fear, the need to control every single dollar, it squeezes the channel shut. And a shut channel cannot flow. If this is you, it looks like not being able to delegate, not being able to spend, not trusting that what goes out actually comes back. And here is the cruel part. The tighter you grip, the less moves. You feel it in your body too. Jaw tight, shoulders up, a little flash of panic when somebody tells you to just let go. It is to open the hand on purpose, without losing the structure. But where exactly you are clamped is different for everybody, and that is the part that takes a real reading.

Quick break, because this connects to what I just said. I built a free thing that tells you which of these three blocks is actually yours, in about a minute. It is called the Money-Block Diagnosis. You answer a few honest questions, and I tell you where your money is stuck and one thing you can start working on today. It is free, there is nothing to buy to see your result. The link is in the description and the pinned comment below. Go find your block, then come right back, because the next two are the ones most people get wrong.

Now, the second block. I call this one the Scatter. This is the person whose money will not move because they are spread too thin. Their energy is running in twenty directions at once, and energy that scattered does not actually go anywhere. Five ideas, five half-built offers, five tabs open. None of them ever gets enough force behind it to actually pay. If this is you, the problem is not that you are lazy. You have plenty of hustle. The problem is that you cannot land on one thing, and every shiny new idea pulls you a little further apart. [FILL IN: a short real example of a Scatterer who picked one channel and what changed.] The fix sounds simple and it is hard to do. You find the one channel your money should pour into, and you let the rest go. Point all that force in one direction, and the money can turn around faster than you would believe.

And the third block, this is the one I see most in people who have been at it a long time. I call this one the Depletion. This is when your money will not move because the tank is empty. There is nothing left to push it with. You have been pouring out and never refilling, and a dead battery cannot run anything, no matter how hard you try. If this is you, it is not a discipline problem. You are just spent. And money cannot move through somebody who is running on fumes. Pushing harder only empties you faster. [FILL IN: a short real example of someone depleted who refilled first.] The work here is the opposite of everything you have been told. Before you try to move anything, you refill the reservoir. You figure out what is draining you and you plug that leak first. People in this spot come back quicker than they expect, because the money was always there. It just had nothing to flow through.

So those are the three. The Grip, the Scatter, and the Depletion. And here is the honest part. You can probably feel which one is yours right now. That tightening in your stomach as I described one of them, that is the tell. That is your body telling you where the block is. The next step is just to look at it clearly, and that is what the Money-Block Diagnosis is for. It is free, it is in the description, go find yours.
