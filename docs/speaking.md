# Speaking and Booking

Zachary Lui teaches classical Chinese medicine as the magickal tradition it grew out of. He is a Wu (巫) and Daoist priest of the Mao Shan Shangqing (茅山上清) transmission, and has apprenticed with his teachers since 2008. He has lectured at Penn State University, presented at the Saam Diagnostic Conference and the World Tai Chi & Qigong Summit, and teaches yearly at Toronto or Hamilton Pagan Pride.

Past appearances are on the [Press](/press) page.

---

## Talks

Each talk runs as a 60 to 90 minute lecture, or expands into a half-day or full-day workshop with qigong practice.

**The Medicine Was Magick.** How Chinese medicine and Daoist magick worked as one practice, how the two were separated in the 1950s, and what the classical texts still carry of the older art.

**Memory Palaces for the Channels.** Points, patterns and pulses learned through myth, character etymology and the art of memory. The method behind CCM is Magick, taught hands-on.

**Neigong: Taking Back Your Power.** Neigong is the internal work inside the qigong forms. Each posture and breath becomes the anchor for a state, mapped across the five senses, with the Yinfujing (陰符經) as the frame. Taught standing, with everyone practising.

**Spirits, Ritual and the Wu.** Daoist and Esoteric Buddhist ritual as living practice: the Wu, the spirits of the Chinese tradition, and where Asian folk practice meets pagan community.

## Formats and fees

- Virtual lecture, up to 90 minutes
- In-person lecture or conference keynote
- Half-day or full-day workshop with qigong practice
- Podcast and press interviews, no fee

Talks start at $750 + HST. Workshops and keynotes are quoted per event. Email for a rate card.

**Ticketed workshops.** Bookstores, temples and community venues can host a ticketed workshop in place of a flat fee: 70% of ticket sales, with a $300 minimum.

**Travel.** Events outside Toronto add rail travel, local transport and lodging at cost.

---

## Booking terms

A 50% deposit holds the date. The balance is due on the day of the event. Cancellations within 14 days of the event keep the deposit.

Lead time: four weeks for talks, eight weeks for workshops.
