Entry 01, from the Index

Divination

Reading one surface for information it has no business carrying. The receipt: 脈象, pulse image, not pulse rate. 象 is the character the Yijing uses for what a diviner reads out of a hexagram.

Sources: Nigel Wiseman and Feng Ye, A Practical Dictionary of Chinese Medicine (Paradigm, 1998); Elisabeth Hsu, Pulse Diagnosis in Early Chinese Medicine (Cambridge University Press, 2010).
